HOW TO PLAY

  • Don't let them get pass you. Anything that reaches the left it's close to the multiverse destruction.
  • You have 3 lives. A hit costs one, with a moment of invulnerability after.
  • Earn more. Every 5,000 points is an extra life, up to five, and the odd drop is one outright.
  • Chain kills without letting anything through to build your multiplier.
  • Grab the drops for rapid fire, a shield, or a screen-clearing blast.
  • Watch for the Latverian Witches. Doom's coven. Each fights its own way, and none of them walk past you — they stop and hold until they are put down.
  • Every fifth wave is a boss. A Sentinel Prime first. Doom himself at wave ten.
  • Thor picks his weapon. Stormbreaker earths chained lightning; Mjolnir is thrown and comes back.
