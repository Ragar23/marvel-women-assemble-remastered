The space dogs are coming for the Infinity Stones. Hold the line.

CHOOSE YOUR HERO

HOW TO PLAY

  • Protect the Stones. Anything that reaches the left edge damages them.
  • You have 3 lives. A hit costs one, with a moment of invulnerability after.
  • Chain kills without letting anything through to build your multiplier.
  • Grab the drops for rapid fire, a shield, or a screen-clearing blast.
  • Survive the waves. Every fifth one, Thanos comes for you.
